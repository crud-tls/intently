Pawse for Chrome is the browser extension of Pawse. In the Chrome Web Store it is still listed as
**"Intently - Intentful Browsing"** until it is renamed. It shows a gentle pause and timer on the
websites you choose and tracks the time you spend on them. The Android app has
[its own policy](/privacy).

**Developer:** MD Sadakat Hussain Fahad, publishing as Intently ·
**Contact:** [pawse@liveintently.app](mailto:pawse@liveintently.app)

## The short version

- Your tracked sites, limits, goals and statistics are stored in your browser.
- The extension sends **usage analytics** to Google Analytics, including the domains of the
  sites you chose to track and how long you spent on them.
- Signing in and cloud sync are optional.
- No ads, and we don't sell your data.

## What stays in your browser

- The **websites you chose to track**, your limits, goals and settings.
- **Sessions on those sites:** when you visited, for how long, and how you responded to each
  pause.
- Statistics, streaks and quest progress built from those sessions.

These are kept in Chrome's extension storage. Your settings use Chrome's sync storage, so if you
use Chrome Sync they follow your Google account to your other browsers; Google handles that sync.

The extension only acts on the sites you add. It does not record your general browsing history.

## Information that leaves your browser

### Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

The extension sends events to Google Analytics, tied to a random ID created when you install it:
session start and end on tracked sites (the site's domain, a timestamp and the duration), pauses
shown and your choice, and use of features such as goals and settings.

### Sign-in and sync (optional)

You can sign in with Google. Your goals, statistics and settings are then synced to a database
hosted by **Supabase** so you can restore them. We receive your email address and Google account
identifier, never your password.

## Permissions

- **Access to websites** (`<all_urls>`), **tabs**, **active tab:** to recognise when you open a
  site you chose to track and show the pause on it.
- **Storage:** to keep your settings and statistics.
- **Alarms, idle, notifications:** for timers, to stop counting while you are away, and for
  reminders.
- **Context menus:** to add the current site from the right-click menu.
- **Identity:** for the optional Google sign-in.

## Sharing

We don't sell or rent your data or use it for advertising. Data reaches only Google (Analytics,
Chrome Sync, Google sign-in) and Supabase (optional sync), acting on our behalf, and anyone the
law requires us to disclose it to. Our use of data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data
Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Retention and deletion

Removing the extension deletes the data stored in your browser. Synced data stays until you ask
us to delete it at [pawse@liveintently.app](mailto:pawse@liveintently.app). Google Analytics
keeps events for up to 14 months.

## Your rights, children, changes

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data by emailing
[pawse@liveintently.app](mailto:pawse@liveintently.app). The extension is not directed at
children under 13. Changes are posted here with a new date.
