This policy covers the Intently website at **liveintently.app** only. Each app has its own policy

**Contact:** [support@liveintently.app](mailto:support@liveintently.app)

## What we collect

- **Contact form:** your name, email address, the app you're asking about and your message. We
  use them only to reply, and they're delivered to our inbox through **Resend**, an email
  provider.
- **Server logs:** the site runs on **Cloudflare**, which processes your IP address and basic
  request details to serve and protect the site.

The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising trackers, and doesn't sell data.

## Retention

Messages stay in our inbox for as long as needed to help you, and you can ask us to delete them.
Cloudflare keeps request logs according to its own retention policy.

## Your rights

Email [support@liveintently.app](mailto:support@liveintently.app) to access or delete what you
sent us. Changes to this policy are posted here with a new date.
