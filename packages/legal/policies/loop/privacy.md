Loop helps you organise recurring tasks, fit them into your free time and get reminded when
they're due.

**Developer:** MD Sadakat Hussain Fahad, publishing as Intently ·
**Package:** `dev.sadakat.loop` ·
**Contact:** [loop@liveintently.app](mailto:loop@liveintently.app)

## The short version

Loop collects nothing. Everything you enter stays on your phone, and the app has no internet
permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no analytics, no crash
reporting and no advertising.

## What Loop stores on your phone

- **Tasks:** name, duration, how often it repeats, priority, colour, and whether it is active.
- **Schedule entries:** when each task is placed, start and end times, and whether you completed
  or skipped it.
- **Time blocks:** the recurring periods you mark as available, such as work hours.
- **Preferences:** onboarding status, notification and reminder settings, buffer time, and
  morning preference.

All of it is kept in a local database in Loop's private storage, which other apps can't read.
Uninstalling Loop, or clearing its data in Android Settings, deletes everything.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`): to remind you about upcoming tasks. Without it the app
  still works; you just won't get reminders.
- **Start at boot** (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`): to re-schedule your reminders after the phone
  restarts.

Loop doesn't ask for internet access, contacts, camera, microphone, location or files.

## Sharing

Loop shares no data with anyone, because it sends none.

## Your rights

All your data is visible and editable in the app, and you can delete it by clearing the app's
data or uninstalling. Because nothing reaches us, there is nothing for us to access, correct or
delete on our side.

## Children

Loop collects no information from anyone, including children.
