Our Commitment to Privacy

Intently is built with privacy as a core principle. Your data stays local on your device by default. Cloud sync is completely optional - the extension works fully without an account. We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising.

Chrome Extension Permissions Explained

Chrome displays permission warnings when you install extensions. Here's exactly what each permission does and why we need it:

1. "Read your browsing history" (tabs permission)

Chrome Warning: "Read your browsing history"

Why We Need It: To track time spent on websites you choose to monitor

What We Actually Access: Only the current active tab's URL and title - NOT your full browsing history

What We DON'T Access: Past browsing history, browsing from other tabs, incognito browsing, passwords, or form data

Your Control: You choose which websites to track. We only monitor sites you explicitly add to your tracking list.

2. "Display notifications" (notifications permission)

Chrome Warning: "Display notifications"

Why We Need It: To show optional usage alerts and goal reminders

Your Control: Notifications are optional and can be completely disabled in extension settings

3. "Know your email address" (identity permission)

Chrome Warning: "Know your email address"

Why We Need It: For optional Google Sign-In to enable cloud sync

Important: This permission is ONLY used when you explicitly click "Sign in with Google"

Your Control: Completely optional - the extension works fully offline without signing in

4. "Read and change all your data on all websites" (host_permissions: <all_urls>)

⚠️ Chrome Warning: "Read and change all your data on all websites"

Why This Warning Appears: Chrome shows this scary warning for ANY extension that can display content on web pages

What We Actually Do:

  • Display intervention overlays ONLY on websites you configure for tracking
  • Detect when you visit a tracked website to start/stop timers
  • Show mindfulness prompts when you open distracting sites

What We DON'T Do:

  • ❌ We do NOT read website content (no passwords, form data, or personal information)
  • ❌ We do NOT modify websites (except showing our own intervention screens)
  • ❌ We do NOT track all your browsing (only user-configured sites)
  • ❌ We do NOT inject ads or change search results

Your Control: You choose which websites to track. We ONLY interact with sites on your tracking list.

5. Other Permissions (No Chrome Warnings)

storage - Save your goals and statistics locally in Chrome's secure storage

alarms - Schedule periodic tracking checks for accurate time monitoring

idle - Detect when you're away from computer to pause tracking (more accurate statistics)

activeTab - Detect when you visit a tracked website (no access to other tabs)

contextMenus - Add right-click menu option to snooze interventions

Information We Collect

Local Storage (Always Active - Stored on Your Device)

The following data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's storage API and never leaves your computer unless you enable cloud sync:

  • Website URLs: Only for sites you choose to track
  • Time Tracking: Time spent on tracked websites
  • Daily Usage Statistics: Aggregated daily usage data
  • Goals and Limits: Daily time limits you set for yourself
  • Settings and Preferences: Extension configuration
  • Session History: Last 100 browsing sessions (automatically cleaned up)
  • Intervention Interactions: How you respond to mindfulness prompts
  • Streaks and Achievements: Progress toward your goals

Cloud Sync (Optional - User Must Enable)

Cloud sync is completely optional. The extension works fully offline. If you choose to sign in with Google:

  • Email Address: Used for authentication only
  • Synced Data: Goals, statistics, settings, session history (encrypted)
  • Authentication Tokens: Securely stored to maintain your session (7-day expiration)

What We DON'T Collect

  • ❌ Full browsing history (only user-configured tracked sites)
  • ❌ Website content, passwords, or form data
  • ❌ Search queries or downloads
  • ❌ Bookmarks or saved passwords
  • ❌ Browsing from other tabs or windows
  • ❌ Incognito/private browsing activity
  • ❌ Personal information without explicit consent
  • ❌ Credit card or payment information

How We Use Your Information

Local Data Usage (No Internet Required)

Your locally stored data is used exclusively to:

  • Track Progress: Monitor time spent on websites you choose to track
  • Display Statistics: Show personalized usage insights and charts
  • Show Interventions: Determine when to display mindfulness prompts
  • Save Preferences: Remember your settings and goals
  • Calculate Streaks: Track achievements and goal progress

Cloud Sync Data (If Enabled)

  • Backup: Securely backup your data
  • Multi-Device Sync: Access your goals and stats across devices
  • Restore Data: Recover data if you reinstall the extension

Third-Party Services

Firebase Analytics (Optional - Anonymous)

Purpose: Anonymous usage analytics to improve extension features

Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Identifiable Data: None - all analytics are completely anonymous

Your Control: Can be disabled in extension settings

Data Retention

Local Data

  • Session History: Last 100 sessions stored (older sessions automatically deleted)

Cloud Data (If Sync Enabled)

  • Active Account: Data stored until you delete your account
