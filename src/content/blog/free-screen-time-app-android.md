# Best Free Screen Time Apps for Android: Privacy-First Options

You don't need to pay a subscription to manage your screen time on Android. Several genuinely free apps offer features that match or exceed their paid competitors — and the best ones do it without collecting your data.

This guide covers the top free Android screen time options, from mindful pause apps to built-in tools, with a focus on which ones respect your privacy and which ones actually help change behavior.

## What to Look for in a Free Screen Time App

Before comparing options, here's what separates a good free screen time app from a mediocre one:

- **Actually free** — no essential features locked behind a paywall
- **Privacy-respecting** — ideally no account required, offline operation, minimal data collection
- **Effective intervention** — goes beyond just showing you numbers; helps you change behavior
- **Low friction to use** — set it up once and it works without constant fiddling

With those criteria in mind, here are the best options.

## 1. Intently — Best Overall Free Option

**Price:** Completely free, no premium tier
**Privacy:** Fully offline, no account, zero data collection
**Platforms:** Android + Chrome extension

Intently takes a mindful approach to screen time. Instead of blocking apps or setting rigid time limits, it creates a brief pause before you open tracked apps. You see a full-screen message — one you've written yourself — that gives you a moment to decide consciously whether to proceed.

### What makes it stand out:

- **Custom interventions** — Write your own pause messages for each app. A pointed question for Instagram, a breathing exercise for Reddit, a goal reminder for YouTube.
- **Complete privacy** — No account creation, no internet required, no data ever leaves your device. This isn't just a privacy policy claim — the app literally never connects to the internet.
- **Usage analytics** — Daily, weekly, and monthly breakdowns stored entirely on your device. See how often you proceed vs. turn back.
- **Gamification** — Streaks and milestones keep you motivated over time.
- **Chrome extension** — Manage distracting websites on desktop too. Same mindful pause, same custom messages.
- **Widgets** — Home screen widgets for quick stats without opening the app.

Every feature is available for free. No premium tier, no trial period, no feature gates.

**Best for:** Users who want a full-featured, privacy-first screen time toolkit that changes habits through awareness rather than restriction.

## 2. Google Digital Wellbeing — Best Built-In Option

**Price:** Free (pre-installed on most Android phones)
**Privacy:** Data stays on device, but tied to Google account
**Platforms:** Android

Digital Wellbeing comes pre-installed on most Android phones running Android 9+. It provides basic screen time stats, app timers, Wind Down mode (grayscale at bedtime), and Focus mode to temporarily pause distracting apps.

### What it does well:

- **Zero setup** — Already on your phone. Go to Settings > Digital Wellbeing.
- **App timers** — Set daily limits for specific apps. When the timer expires, the app icon grays out.
- **Wind Down** — Gradually shifts your screen to grayscale at bedtime.
- **Focus mode** — Temporarily pause distracting apps during work or study.

### What it lacks:

- No mindful interventions or custom prompts
- Limited analytics beyond basic daily stats
- App timers are easy to bypass (just tap "use anyway")
- No browser extension for desktop habits
- Tied to your Google account

**Best for:** Users who want basic monitoring without installing anything new.

## 3. StayFree — Best for Detailed Stats

**Price:** Free tier with ads; premium removes ads and adds features
**Privacy:** Collects some analytics; account optional but recommended
**Platforms:** Android + Chrome

StayFree provides detailed screen time statistics with daily, weekly, and historical usage data. The free tier includes basic usage tracking, app-by-app breakdowns, and overuse alerts.

### What it does well:

- **Detailed usage reports** — Charts and graphs showing usage patterns over time
- **Overuse alerts** — Notifications when you exceed set thresholds
- **Chrome extension** — Track website usage alongside app usage

### What it lacks:

- Free tier has ads
- No mindful pause interventions
- Privacy is moderate — some data collection for analytics
- Advanced features require premium subscription

**Best for:** Data-oriented users who want detailed usage statistics and don't mind ads.

## 4. FlowBuddy — Best Minimal Open-Source Option

**Price:** Completely free, open-source
**Privacy:** Open-source code, minimal data
**Platforms:** Android only

FlowBuddy is a minimal, open-source app that does one thing: show a breathing animation before you open tracked apps. No tracking, no analytics, no custom messages — just a simple pause.

### What it does well:

- **Open source** — Code is publicly available for anyone to inspect
- **Dead simple** — One feature, no configuration required
- **Lightweight** — Minimal resource usage

### What it lacks:

- No usage tracking or analytics
- No custom messages
- No Chrome extension
- No gamification
- Does one thing only — if that one thing isn't enough, there's nothing else to fall back on

**Best for:** Users who want the absolute simplest possible breathing pause with full code transparency.

## 5. ActionDash — Best Digital Wellbeing Extension

**Price:** Free tier; premium for advanced features
**Privacy:** Moderate — some data collection
**Platforms:** Android

ActionDash extends the built-in Digital Wellbeing experience with more detailed statistics, usage history beyond 24 hours, and additional tracking metrics.

### What it does well:

- **Extended history** — Usage data beyond Google's 24-hour window
- **Detailed breakdowns** — Screen unlocks, notification counts, per-app timing
- **Clean interface** — Well-designed dashboard

### What it lacks:

- No mindful interventions or pause features
- Some features require premium
- Primarily a monitoring tool, not a behavior-change tool

**Best for:** Users who like Digital Wellbeing's approach but want deeper statistics.

**Want to change habits through awareness?** Choose Intently. The mindful pause approach catches unconscious app opens and turns them into conscious decisions.

## Why Privacy Matters in Screen Time Apps

It's worth pausing on the privacy question. Screen time apps, by nature, know intimate details about your behavior — which apps you use, how often, when, and for how long. That data is a goldmine for advertisers and data brokers.

Some free screen time apps monetize by collecting and selling this behavioral data. Others show targeted ads based on your usage patterns. The irony of a screen time app that profits from your data should give you pause.

Apps like Intently take a different approach: everything stays on your device. No account creation means no profile to build. No internet connection means no data to transmit. You get the insights you need without giving up the information you'd rather keep private.

When choosing a free screen time app, check: Does it require an account? Does it need internet access? What does its privacy policy actually say? The best free apps are the ones that stay free because they don't need your data to sustain themselves.
