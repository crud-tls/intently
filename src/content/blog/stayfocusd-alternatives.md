# Best StayFocusd Alternatives (2026 Review)

StayFocusd has been the go-to free Chrome blocking extension since 2011, and its Nuclear Option — an unbypassable block on all distracting sites — remains one of the most powerful enforcement features available. For pure, no-nonsense time limits in Chrome, it still works.

But the extension is showing its age. The interface hasn't had a major refresh in years, the settings can be confusing to configure, and the approach is purely restriction-based — it doesn't build the awareness or self-regulation skills that create lasting change. If you've been using StayFocusd and want something different — whether that's a more modern blocker, a more flexible rule system, or a fundamentally different approach — here are your best options.

## The Alternatives

### Intently Chrome Extension — Mindful Awareness, Not Blocking

**Price**: Free | **Approach**: Mindful pause before sites load

Intently takes a fundamentally different approach from StayFocusd. Instead of blocking sites after a time limit, it creates a brief moment of awareness before the site loads — a full-screen prompt that asks you to take a breath and decide whether you really want to be there.

This matters because StayFocusd's blocking doesn't change the underlying habit. When your daily limit resets at midnight, old patterns are right where you left them. Intently builds awareness that persists because each pause is a practice rep for conscious decision-making.

There's no time limit, no scheduling, no Nuclear Option. It's purely about creating a moment of choice at the decision point. For people who want to build lasting self-regulation rather than outsource willpower to a blocker, this is the right tool.

Rating: 4/5 — Best for building lasting habits. Free, lightweight, and privacy-first. Different philosophy than StayFocusd.

### LeechBlock — Most Flexible Blocking Rules

**Price**: Free | **Approach**: Time-based and schedule-based blocking

LeechBlock is the power user's alternative to StayFocusd. Where StayFocusd gives you a single daily time allowance, LeechBlock lets you create complex multi-rule configurations:

- Block Twitter from 9 AM to 5 PM on weekdays
- Allow Reddit for 15 minutes per hour
- Block everything during custom focus sessions
- Different rules for different days

The rule system is genuinely powerful — more flexible than any other free extension. It's also open-source, which means transparent development and no data collection concerns.

The interface isn't pretty. Configuration requires reading documentation. But if you want granular control over when and how long you can access specific sites, LeechBlock is unmatched.

Rating: 4/5 — The most customizable free blocker. Interface could use modernization.

---

### Cold Turkey (Chrome + Desktop) — Nuclear-Grade Enforcement

**Price**: $39 one-time | **Approach**: Unbreakable blocking

If StayFocusd's Nuclear Option appeals to you but you want something more comprehensive, Cold Turkey takes enforcement to the extreme. Once you start a block, you genuinely cannot disable it — not by restarting Chrome, not by reinstalling the extension, not by editing system files.

Cold Turkey works primarily as a desktop application (Windows/Mac) with a Chrome component. This means it blocks distracting sites across all browsers, not just Chrome — closing the "I'll just open Firefox" loophole. The scheduling is powerful, and the one-time $39 price means no ongoing subscription.

The trade-off: it's the most restrictive option here and purely enforcement-based. Zero awareness-building. But if you need guaranteed distraction-free periods and StayFocusd's enforcement isn't strong enough, Cold Turkey is the next level.

Rating: 3.5/5 — The strongest enforcement available. Desktop app + Chrome coverage closes bypass routes.

---

### Freedom — Cross-Device Blocking

**Price**: ~$40/year | **Approach**: Block sites and apps across all devices

Freedom extends blocking beyond Chrome to cover your phone, tablet, and all browsers simultaneously. Block Twitter in Chrome, and it's also blocked on your phone and in Safari. This eliminates the most common bypass strategy: switching devices.

The "Locked Sessions" feature prevents you from disabling blocks once started. Session scheduling is flexible, and there's a "Recurring Sessions" feature for daily work routines.

The downsides: subscription pricing (~$40/year vs. StayFocusd's free), traffic routing through Freedom's servers (privacy concern for some users), and — like all blockers — no awareness-building. But if your issue is device-switching to get around Chrome blockers, Freedom is the comprehensive solution.

Rating: 3.5/5 — Best for multi-device consistency. The subscription model is justified if you need cross-device enforcement.

---

### BlockSite — Simple URL Blocking

**Price**: Free (limited) / Premium | **Approach**: URL blocklist

BlockSite is StayFocusd stripped to its simplest form: add URLs, and they're blocked. No time limits, no scheduling, no Nuclear Option. Just a blocklist.

The free version covers a limited number of sites. Premium adds scheduling, password protection, more sites, and mobile apps. It's the most straightforward option if you want dead-simple "this site is off-limits" blocking.

The limitations are obvious: no nuance, no time-based allowances, and basic blocking without any awareness-building. But simplicity is its own virtue for people who want zero configuration.

Rating: 3/5 — The simplest option. No frills, no fuss.

---

### News Feed Eradicator + Unhook YouTube — Surgical Approach

**Price**: Free | **Approach**: Remove addictive features, keep useful ones

This isn't a single extension but a combination approach that's worth considering as a StayFocusd alternative. Instead of blocking entire sites, these extensions surgically remove the specific features that drive compulsive use:

- **News Feed Eradicator** removes the infinite scroll feed from Facebook, Twitter, Reddit, and other platforms while keeping profiles, messages, and groups accessible.
- **Unhook YouTube** removes recommendations, autoplay, trending, and other algorithm-driven features while keeping search and subscriptions.

This is philosophically different from both blocking and pausing. You can still use these platforms for their legitimate purposes — you just can't lose yourself in the algorithmic feed. For people who need social media for work or connection but waste hours in the feed, this combination is transformative.

Rating: 4/5 — The smartest approach for people who need platforms but not the feed. Pairs well with Intently.

- **Zero cost**: Completely free with no premium tier.

## Picking the Right One

**Want StayFocusd but more flexible**: LeechBlock gives you complex scheduling and per-site time rules.

**Want StayFocusd but stronger**: Cold Turkey's desktop app + Chrome component is genuinely unbreakable.

**Want something fundamentally different**: Intently builds awareness and self-regulation instead of enforcing restrictions. Better for lasting change.

**Want to keep sites but remove feeds**: News Feed Eradicator + Unhook YouTube surgically remove the addictive parts.

**Want cross-device coverage**: Freedom blocks across all devices and browsers.

The best approach for many people combines tools: Intently for daily mindful awareness, plus News Feed Eradicator for social media, with StayFocusd's Nuclear Option or Cold Turkey reserved for when you need guaranteed enforcement during critical work periods.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use StayFocusd and Intently together?

Yes. They take different approaches (blocking vs. awareness) that complement each other. Use StayFocusd's time limits or Nuclear Option for specific enforcement periods, and Intently's mindful pauses for the rest of your browsing.

### What happens when StayFocusd's daily limit resets?

Your allowed time resets at midnight (or a custom time you set). All blocked sites become accessible again until you use up the new day's allowance. This is a structural limitation — it doesn't change the urge to visit those sites, just temporarily prevents access.

### Is Cold Turkey really unbreakable?

On desktop, effectively yes. You cannot disable it during an active block by any normal means — including restarting, uninstalling, or editing system files. On Chrome alone (without the desktop app), it's bypassable like any extension.

### Which is better for students?

For studying: Cold Turkey or StayFocusd's Nuclear Option during study sessions provides guaranteed enforcement. For building long-term habits: Intently builds the self-regulation skills that will serve you beyond school. Ideally, use both — enforcement during study blocks, awareness the rest of the time.
