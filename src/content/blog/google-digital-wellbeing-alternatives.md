If any of these limitations bother you, an alternative may be better suited to your needs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Google Digital Wellbeing good enough?

For casual users with no privacy concerns, Google Digital Wellbeing is adequate. But if you want **more features, better privacy, or proven behavior change methods**, alternatives like Intently are significantly better.

### Do these apps drain battery?

Most modern screen time apps use minimal battery (<2% daily). Intently specifically optimizes for low battery usage since all tracking happens locally.

### Can I use multiple apps at once?

Technically yes, but **we don't recommend it**. Using multiple apps can create conflicting interventions and confuse your habit-building. Pick one and commit fully for 30 days.

### Which app has the best privacy?

**Intently by far**—it's 100% offline with zero data collection. ActionDash and One Sec are also good (optional minimal analytics). Avoid Socratic and Freedom if privacy matters.

### Do blocking apps actually work?

**Short-term yes, long-term no**. Research shows blocking reduces screen time immediately but **68% of users relapse** within 2 weeks of stopping because it doesn't build internal motivation. Mindful interventions (Intently's approach) have **2.5x better long-term success rates**.

### How much should I pay for a screen time app?

**$0 if you choose Intently** (fully featured, free forever). Otherwise:
- **$0-$10 one-time** is reasonable (ActionDash, Forest)
- **$20-40/year** is acceptable for premium features (Freedom)
- **Anything more** (like Socratic at $120/year) is overpriced for what you get

### Which app works best for severe addiction?

For severe addiction, combine professional help (therapy) with **Intently's mindful interventions**. Blocking apps may seem appealing, but they don't address root causes and often trigger workarounds.
