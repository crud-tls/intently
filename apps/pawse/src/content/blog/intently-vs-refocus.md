# Intently vs Refocus: Every-Launch Pause or End-of-Budget Reminder?

Intently and Refocus both help you manage screen time, but they intervene at completely different moments. Intently shows a mindful pause before every app launch — turning each open into a conscious decision. Refocus sets daily time budgets and nudges you when you've used too much.

The difference matters more than it sounds. One approach catches you before the scroll starts. The other lets you scroll freely until a timer runs out.

## Quick Verdict

Intently creates a mindful pause before every app open — customizable prompts, full offline privacy, free with no limits. Refocus sets daily time budgets and sends gentle reminders when you approach your limits. Choose Intently if you want to make every app launch intentional. Choose Refocus if you prefer tracking cumulative time and getting nudged at your limits.

**Bottom line:** If your problem is opening apps without thinking, Intently addresses that directly. If your problem is losing track of how long you've been scrolling, Refocus addresses that.

## How Each App Works

### Intently: Pause Before Every Launch

Every time you open a tracked app, Intently shows a full-screen intervention. You see a message you've written yourself — a question, a goal reminder, a breathing prompt — and you make a conscious choice about whether to proceed.

This happens every time. Not after 30 minutes. Not when you hit a limit. Every single launch. The idea is that most problematic phone use starts with an unconscious app open — your thumb taps Instagram before your brain engages. Intently catches that moment.

You always have the choice to continue. The intervention isn't a block; it's a pause. But that pause is often enough to break the autopilot cycle.

Everything runs offline on your device. No account, no data collection, no internet required.

### Refocus: Budget Your Time, Get Nudged at Limits

Refocus takes a time-management approach. You set daily time budgets for specific apps — say, 30 minutes for social media, 20 minutes for news. Refocus tracks your usage throughout the day and sends gentle reminders when you're approaching your limit.

The first time you open an app, nothing happens. You use it freely. The intervention comes later, when cumulative usage approaches the boundary you set. This approach is less intrusive during normal use but relies on you responding to the nudge when it arrives.

Refocus works on both iOS and Android.

## Where They Differ

### When the Intervention Happens

This is the core difference. Intently intervenes proactively — before every app launch, before any scrolling begins. Refocus intervenes reactively — after you've already been using an app and hit a time threshold.

Why this matters: most people don't struggle with consciously deciding to spend 45 minutes on Instagram. They struggle with the unconscious app open — the reflex that happens dozens of times a day without any thought. By the time a time-budget reminder fires, you've already been scrolling.

On the other hand, some users find that every-launch pauses become too frequent or that they habituate to them. If your main problem is genuinely spending too long in apps (rather than opening them too often), time budgets might address your actual issue more directly.

### Customization

Intently gives you deep control over your interventions. You write custom messages for each app, choose intervention types (questions, affirmations, breathing exercises), and adjust timing. You can evolve your prompts as your goals change.

Refocus's customization centers on time budgets — how many minutes per app per day, and when to receive reminders. The interaction model is simpler but less personalized.

### Privacy

Intently is fully offline. No account, no internet connection, no data collection of any kind. Your usage data stays on your device and never leaves.

Refocus requires an account and collects usage data for its time tracking features. This is standard for time-budget apps, but it means your screen time data exists on external servers.

### Pricing

Intently is completely free — every feature, no premium tier, no subscriptions.

Refocus offers a free tier with core features, with some advanced capabilities behind a premium subscription.

### Platform Support

Refocus has an edge on mobile with both iOS and Android support. Intently covers Android with iOS coming soon, but also offers a Chrome extension for managing website habits on desktop — something Refocus doesn't provide.

## Who Should Choose What

### Choose Intently if:

- Your main problem is opening apps without thinking — the unconscious reflex
- You want every app launch to be a conscious decision
- Privacy is important — you want zero data collection and offline operation
- You want customizable interventions that speak in your own voice
- You need a Chrome extension for website habits too
- You want everything free with no premium gates

### Choose Refocus if:

- Your main problem is spending too long in apps once you're in them
- You prefer setting time budgets and getting nudged at your limits
- You need iOS support right now
- You find every-launch pauses too frequent or intrusive
- You're comfortable with a freemium model and cloud-based tracking
- A time-management approach fits your mental model better than a mindfulness approach

## Different Problems, Different Tools

The choice between Intently and Refocus often comes down to diagnosing your actual problem:

**If you pick up your phone 80 times a day without thinking**, Intently addresses the root cause — the unconscious app launch. A time budget won't help because the problem isn't duration, it's frequency.

**If you open apps deliberately but then lose track of time for hours**, Refocus addresses the root cause — losing awareness of passing time. An every-launch pause won't help much if you consciously choose to open the app each time.

Many people have both problems. If you're unsure which is primary, try tracking your phone pickups for a day (most phones have this stat in settings). If the number is high (50+), frequency is likely your main issue and Intently is the better fit. If pickups are moderate but total screen time is very high, duration is the issue and Refocus might serve you better.
